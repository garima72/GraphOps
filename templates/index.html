How to interpret this view

  • Blast radius: nodes reachable within three hops. Ranked by illustrative risk score (criticality × avg edge weight / hops).
  • Attack path: shortest relationship path under current mode and time filter. Not proof of exploitability.
  • Containment: structural simulation of isolating intermediary nodes. Never guarantees containment.
  • Choke point: high betweenness centrality nodes may connect otherwise separate areas.
  • Alert correlation: sample alerts linked through shared entities.

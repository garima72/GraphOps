# GraphOps — Enhanced Build Summary

**Theme:** Cybersecurity and Technology  
**Challenge:** Graph analytics for proactive threat hunting and blast-radius containment.

## What is built
- Interactive Cytoscape.js force-directed graph (zoom, pan, drag, tooltips, criticality colors, centrality sizing, path/blast highlights).
- Risk-weighted blast radius with transparent formula and breakdown.
- Containment suggestion engine (isolation simulation on selected path).
- Expanded synthetic graph (~40 nodes) with relationship weights and timestamps.
- Directed/undirected modes and timeline filter.
- Play Scenario guided demo.
- Existing blast radius, path, choke-point, and alert workflows preserved and enhanced.
- Automated tests.

## Deliberate omissions
No live SIEM/EDR integration, no automated blocking, no production anomaly detection, no claim of predicting exploitability or guaranteeing containment.

## Data status
All included data is synthetic.

## Limitations
Scores and suggestions are illustrative only. See README for full disclosure.
